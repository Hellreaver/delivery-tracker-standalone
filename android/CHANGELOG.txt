1.4
- When a new version is out, a line at the top of the Log tab says so,
  with a dot on the Settings tab. It never shows during a shift, and "Not
  now" hides it until the next version

1.3
- Targets count from the day you start using the app (or your earliest
  logged shift). Days before that no longer show up, so installing mid-month
  doesn't show you hundreds of dollars behind

1.2
- New installs start with monthly targets of Base $1,000 and Stretch $1,300
- The $/mile badge on each delivery blends from red at your Minimum $/mile,
  through amber halfway, to green at your Good $/mile, instead of three
  fixed colors
- Less battery during a shift: the app's built-in server no longer wakes
  up twice a second while waiting; it only runs when the app asks it something

1.1
- First standalone release. The app runs its own server on the phone, so it
  needs no computer and no signal; everything is stored on the phone
- Settings > Android app: save a backup, restore one, and pick a folder for
  automatic daily backups (the last 14 days are kept)
- The app checks GitHub for a newer version and offers to download it
- Targets are called Base and Stretch
- Editing a saved live shift lists its deliveries newest first, like the Uber
  app, with a button to flip the order
